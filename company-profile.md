# Company Profile

ROCTECH (Roctech Machinery Co., Ltd) — CNC routers and fibre lasers, made in Jinan, China. Lasers sell under the ROCLAS name.

ROCTECH builds CNC routers and fibre laser cutting machines, and has done for 15 years. They're not the biggest name in the category, but they ship to 100+ countries and a fair number of their customers come back and buy again. The facts below are the ones worth leading with when someone asks "who are these guys?"

**Two brands, one company**

**ROCTECH** and **ROCLAS** are two brands of the same manufacturer — Jinan Ruotai CNC Technology Co., Ltd. (若泰数控), headquartered in Jinan, China.

**ROCTECH** is the main brand for CNC routers and machining centers: woodworking routers, ATC nesting machines, edge banders, panel drills, and 4-/5-axis stone, foam and mold machines.

**ROCLAS** is the sub-brand for fibre laser cutting machines — sheet, tube, and combined sheet-and-tube models.

That is why some cases here carry the ROCTECH name and others ROCLAS: same manufacturer, two product-line brands.

### What they're known for

• **Accuracy:** mid-range routers quoted at 0.01 mm positioning / 0.005 mm repeat; 5-axis verified at 0.01 mm under a live 18,000 rpm spindle load.

• **Holding up:** one customer ran a machine 10 hrs/day, 6 days/week for 7+ years and only ever replaced a $200 belt.

• **Saving material:** a cabinet shop dropped scrap from 18% to 7%.

• **Lower running cost:** roughly 15–20% less power than comparable machines.

• **Support:** 1 year of free spare parts, free first-year training, 2-year warranty on core parts, EU/US spare-parts stock.
