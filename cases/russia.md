## One Machine for Sheet and Tube: A Russian Craft-Metal Workshop's 3015 Laser

Craft metal · -10 °C

`Laser 3000W` `Sheet-and-tube` `Low temperature`

**Customer**: Metal crafts manufacturer, Russia

**What they make**: Decorative ornaments, openwork pendants, tube-based components

**Machine**: 3015 integrated sheet-and-tube fibre laser, 3000W

**Working environment**: Runs down to -10 °C, built for Russian winters

### The customer and the job

This is a craft metal shop: ornaments, openwork pendants and tube-based parts. Orders come in small batches with a lot of different designs, and the shop pays Russian rent and winter heating on every square metre. Buying two separate machines was never going to make sense.

### Why two machines did not fit

  * A separate flatbed plus a separate tube cutter eats double floor space, and in Russia that means paying for rent and heating twice.
  * Two machines mean two purchase prices, two freight and customs bills, and two sets of spare parts to keep in stock.
  * Moving sheet and tube between two machines adds handling time to every small custom order.
  * Two machines need two operators, and skilled metal workers in Russia are not cheap.
  * Switching between plate and tube work on separate machines is slow when almost every order is a different design.

### What we recommended

A 3015 sheet-and-tube fibre laser at 3000W. One bed, one control, one operator.

  * Cuts plate, round tube and square or rectangular tube on the same machine, so the shop only buys once.
  * Cuts the floor footprint roughly in half, which matters every winter.
  * Uses the same laser system for both materials, so sheet and tube parts line up properly at assembly.
  * Switches between plate and pipe in about five to ten minutes, with no dismantling.
  * Runs in workshops down to -10 °C, with the electrical and optical systems protected for low-temperature work.

### Core specifications

  * Laser power: 3000W (2000W and 3000W available; 3000W recommended for craft work)
  * Plate working area: 3000 × 1500 mm
  * Tube range: round 20-160 mm diameter; square and rectangular 20 × 20 to 120 × 120 mm
  * Cutting accuracy: ±0.03 mm, kerf ≤ 0.1 mm
  * Max plate cut: carbon steel 10 mm, stainless steel 6 mm, aluminium 3 mm
  * Max tube cut: carbon steel 6 mm, stainless steel 4 mm
  * Control: dedicated sheet-and-tube CNC with a built-in craft image library, DXF/DWG import
  * Drive: full servo motors
  * Footprint: about 4.2 m × 2.2 m

### Questions the customer asked

**Q: What metals will it cut?**
Stainless steel, carbon steel, galvanized sheet, aluminium sheet, decorative copper tube and iron tube, which covers the common raw materials used in metal crafts.

**Q: How does the machine get to Russia, and what about customs?**
It ships in a thickened, moisture-proof wooden crate for sea or land transport. We also provide export documents and a certificate of origin to help with Russian customs clearance.

**Q: Will it fail in cold weather?**
The electrical system and laser are treated for low-temperature operation and run stably down to -10 °C. In winter the shop only needs basic insulation.

**Q: Is switching between sheet and pipe a hassle?**
No parts need to be dismantled. The system switches modes with one click, and the pipe fixtures come off quickly. A full switch takes about five to ten minutes.

**Q: Are spare parts available in Russia?**
Common consumables such as lenses, nozzles and ceramic rings ship in bulk with the machine, and we support international express delivery plus video-guided repair for urgent faults.

**Q: Do the cut pieces need grinding afterwards?**
Thin craft sheet comes off essentially burr-free and can go straight to bending or welding, so grinding is mostly eliminated.

**Q: Can we import our own drawing files?**
Yes. The CNC takes DXF and DWG files, so customer-designed ornaments and pipe shapes can be imported and cut directly.

**Q: How hard is it for workers with no experience to learn?**
The interface is in English and comes with tutorials and video training. A new operator can handle plate and pipe programming on their own in two to three days.
