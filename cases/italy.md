## RCL3015-3000W with Cover and Push-Pull Table: An Italian Sheet-Metal Shop

Industrial / architectural · EU compliance

`Laser 3000W` `Full enclosure` `FDA / CE`

**Country**: Italy

**Industry**: Sheet-metal fabrication, industrial and architectural

**Work**: 1-12 mm stainless for furniture parts and enclosure panels

**Machine**: RCL3015-3000W, full cover, push-pull table

### The customer and the problems

The shop cuts stainless from 1 mm up to 12 mm into custom furniture components and industrial enclosure panels. Three issues were holding it back.

  * The plasma cutter was too slow on 3-6 mm stainless, which sat right in the middle of their work.
  * Cut quality was inconsistent, with dross and jagged edges that meant heavy secondary finishing.
  * The labour inspectorate had flagged the open machine for not meeting EU CE and FDA safety expectations, and fines were a real risk.
  * New operators kept misplacing the cutting start point and wasting the first sheet of the day.
  * The old generic servos and rails broke down often and delayed deliveries to a key automotive customer.

### What we recommended

The RCL3015-3000W with a full cover and push-pull table.

  * A 3000W Raytools source cuts 0-12 mm stainless about three times faster than plasma, with a nitrogen-assisted oxide-free edge.
  * The 3000 × 1500 mm travel matches standard EU sheet sizes exactly, so sheets go straight on without extra shearing.
  * The full enclosure and FDA/CE compliance clear the Italian labour inspection and protect the operators.
  * The Raytools control panel with a border preview lets operators check the cutting path before starting, so the first sheet is not wasted.
  * Shimpo reducers, Taiwan helical rack and rails, and Schneider electrics keep the machine running through continuous shifts.

### Questions the customer asked

**Q: Can this machine cut 20 mm stainless steel?**
Yes, with oxygen assist and a slower speed. If cuts above 15 mm are frequent, a 6000W model is the better choice.

**Q: The parameters mention Taiwan rails OEM for Roclas. Are they genuine?**
They are made by the same Taiwanese foundry to ROCTECH specifications and quality control, so performance is identical.

**Q: Does the push-pull table need a special foundation?**
No. The frame is pre-levelled and just sits on a flat industrial concrete floor.

**Q: What happens if the fume extractor fails mid-cut?**
The Raytools control detects the airflow loss and pauses the cut automatically to protect the optics.

**Q: Is the software available in Italian?**
Yes, the Raytools system supports Italian, English and Chinese.

**Q: Do you supply EU-compliant plugs?**
Yes, the machine ships with Schneider components and standard Schuko/CE plug configuration.

**Q: How long does a table swap take?**
About 30 seconds, using the pneumatic locking mechanism.

**Q: What is the power draw at full load?**
Roughly 12-15 kW/h including the chiller and fume extractor.

**Q: Can it cut aluminium?**
Yes, up to 8 mm with nitrogen and a coated protective lens that is included in the kit.

**Q: Why does Italy need FDA compliance?**
The FDA laser-safety glass meets the strictest CE requirements, so the machine passes Italian labour inspections without a problem.
