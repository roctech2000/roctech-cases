## RCL3015-3000W: How a Malaysian Elevator Fabricator Left Plasma Behind

Elevator · dropped plasma

`Laser 3000W` `Replaced plasma` `Higher output`

**Customer**: Tony, elevator sheet-metal factory, Malaysia

**Machine**: RCL3015-3000W fibre laser

**Work**: Elevator car panels, door frames, brackets, stainless decorative parts

**Materials**: 304 stainless 1-6 mm, carbon steel 2-10 mm, aluminium 1-4 mm

**Budget**: RMB 220,000 - 280,000

### The customer and the workload

Tony runs a mid-size elevator component factory in Malaysia. The shop cuts AISI 304 stainless, carbon steel and some aluminium into car panels, door frames, brackets and decorative pattern parts. Peak season comes hard, and the old plasma setup had become the bottleneck.

### Where plasma was costing them

  * Edges came out rough with heavy slag, so every part was hand-polished. Fine holes and small patterns were hit or miss.
  * Aluminium slagged badly and stainless blackened at the cut, so a lot of decorative work was being outsourced and eating into margin.
  * With all the hand finishing, one plasma cutter managed about 30 sets of parts a day. Peak orders meant late deliveries and lost customers.
  * Thermal distortion on shaped parts ran 0.3-0.5 mm off, assembly gaps did not close, and scrap stayed above 8%.
  * Electrodes and nozzles wore out fast, and the noise and smoke were becoming a workshop problem.

### What we recommended

The RCL3015-3000W. The 3000W source matches their material range without paying for power the shop would never use.

  * 3000W cuts 10 mm carbon steel, 6 mm stainless and 4 mm aluminium cleanly, covering the full thickness range they process.
  * Positioning accuracy of ±0.03 mm, repeat accuracy of ±0.02 mm and a minimum kerf of 0.15 mm bring the surface roughness to Ra ≤ 12.5 µm and remove about 80% of the polishing work.
  * The standard 3015 table takes 3 m × 1.5 m sheets, the common size in elevator work, with no custom oversize table needed.
  * 120 m/min rapid travel and 1.5G acceleration cut thin sheet about three times faster than plasma, pushing daily output past 80 sets.

### Questions the customer asked

**Q: Can the 3000W cut 6 mm stainless with a smooth finish?**
Yes. With high-pressure oxygen assist there is no slag adhesion, and Ra stays within 12.5 µm, so decorative panels need no secondary polishing.

**Q: Will it break down running ten hours a day?**
The laser source has a one-year warranty, the head and servo motors are first-tier brands, and the bed is stress-relief annealed. With normal maintenance it runs ten hours a day with under four hours of maintenance downtime a month.

**Q: How much labour does it save compared with plasma?**
Plasma parts currently need five to ten minutes of hand polishing each. The laser removes 70-80% of that, worth two or three polishers a month.

**Q: What comes with the machine, and what are the running consumables?**
It ships with a cutting head, several nozzle sizes, protective lenses, air lines and a tool kit. Regular consumables run about 300-500 Malaysian Ringgit a month, far below plasma electrode and nozzle spend.

**Q: How much floor space does it need?**
The machine is 4.6 m × 2.45 m, needs about 0.8 m of clearance around it, and takes roughly 18 square metres in total. It fits the existing workshop without expansion.
