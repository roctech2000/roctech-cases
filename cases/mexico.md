## 6000W Fibre Laser for a Mexican Metal Fabrication Shop

Metal · high power

`Laser 6000W` `Medium/thick plate`

**Country**: Mexico

**Industry**: Metal fabrication: carbon steel, stainless steel, aluminium

**Machine**: 6000W fibre laser, 1500 × 3000 mm working area

**The ask**: Higher cutting speed, lower labour cost

### The situation

The shop was falling behind on orders. Its existing equipment could not keep carbon, stainless and aluminium moving fast enough to hit delivery dates, and the factory wanted more throughput without adding headcount.

### What we recommended

A 6000W fibre laser. At that power, medium and thick plate moves quickly, and thin sheet runs at high speed.

  * 6000W source, with MAX, Raycus or IPG as options.
  * Raytools cutting head and CypCut control system.
  * 1500 × 3000 mm working area, with other sizes available.
  * Positioning accuracy ±0.03 mm, repeat accuracy ±0.02 mm.
  * Cuts carbon steel to around 25 mm, plus stainless steel, aluminium, brass and galvanized sheet.

### Questions the customer asked

**Q: What materials can it cut?**
Carbon steel, stainless steel, aluminium, copper and galvanized sheet.

**Q: How do files get to the machine?**
Files transfer over an Ethernet cable, and the operator runs the job on site.

**Q: How thick a steel plate can the 6000W cut?**
It depends on the material and the cut, but carbon steel goes up to about 25 mm.

**Q: Will the laser damage the base plate?**
Fire-resistant bricks are installed under the cutting area to take the heat and prevent fire damage.

**Q: How does batch processing work?**
Batch jobs use a set MARK point 1 position, which keeps a consistent processing origin for every piece.
