## RC3015L 3000W: A Compact Fibre Laser for a Uruguayan Sheet-Metal Shop

Sheet · value for money

`Laser 3000W` `Open type` `One-person loading`

**Country**: Uruguay

**Work**: Thin sheet metal cutting

**Machine**: RC3015L 3000W open fibre laser, 3000 × 1500 mm

**The ask**: High value for money, one-person loading

### The customer

The factory runs a small workforce and a small workshop but still has to process a large amount of raw sheet. The owner wanted a cost-effective 3 kW machine for thin sheet and a way to load it without adding extra hands.

### What we recommended

The open-type RC3015L 3000W. It keeps the purchase price down, cuts thin sheet fast, and with a vacuum lifter arm a single operator can handle loading, which removes most of the labour cost.

### Machine build

  * RC3015L 3000W, 220V / 60 Hz / 3PH
  * Travel size 3000 × 1500 × 150 mm
  * Blade table on a welded structure
  * Raytools cutting head and Raytools control
  * 3000W MAX fibre laser source
  * Inovance servo drive system with motor reducers
  * Taiwan Kingtec square rails and YYC helical rack and pinion, both OEM for ROCTECH
  * Schneider (France) electrical components
  * HL-3000W water chiller
  * Fume extraction blower and hoses

### Questions the customer asked

**Q: What is the warranty?**
Two years for ordinary accessories and one year for the important accessories.

**Q: What if we do not know how to use it after delivery?**
We provide 24-hour online support, operation manuals and engineer on-site guidance.

**Q: Can the machine be customized?**
Yes. Colour, specification, system, motor, spindle and other details can be changed to the customer's requirements.

**Q: How is it delivered to Uruguay?**
Both sea and land transport are available.

**Q: How should we maintain it?**
Keep the machine clean after cutting, check the wires and transmission parts for rust and wear on a schedule, and lubricate regularly.

ROCTECH kept it open and affordable so one person could run the floor.
