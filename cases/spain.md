## RC3015T-3000W: Sheet and Tube in One Machine for a Spanish Fabricator

Steel structure · replaced two

**Country**: Spain

**Industry**: Metal and steel-structure fabrication

**Machine**: RC3015T-3000W combined sheet-and-tube fibre laser

**Supplies**: Construction, fitness equipment, agricultural machinery makers

### The customer and the job

The shop works out of a single-storey building and cuts flat sheet and tube at the same time. The finished parts go to local makers in construction, fitness equipment and agricultural machinery, and sheet and tube are often blanked together in one batch.

### What the two-machine setup was costing

  * They already owned a flatbed laser and a plasma tube cutter. Two machines meant double floor space, double procurement and double maintenance, and the tube cutter sat idle whenever tube orders dropped.
  * Moving sheet and tube between two machines stretched out every batch and made rush orders impossible.
  * The old equipment burned edges on stainless and thin plate, so that work went outside, came back slow and raised external processing cost.
  * Two control systems and two spare-parts shelves made fault finding slower, and plasma electrodes and nozzles wore out every month.

### What we recommended

The RC3015T-3000W. One machine does both jobs and does them on the same bed.

  * Integrated sheet and tube processing replaces two machines, cuts floor occupancy by half and lowers the initial investment.
  * The 3000W MAX source cuts 16 mm carbon steel, 8 mm stainless and 6 mm aluminium, covering every conventional thickness in their orders.
  * Repeat positioning of ±0.02 mm, with holes and intersecting tube cuts formed in one pass, cutting polishing labour by about 70%.
  * CE certified with a wide-voltage 3-phase 380V/50Hz input that stays stable within ±10% grid fluctuation, matching Spanish factory power.
  * MAX source, Raytools head and Leadshine servos, with standard spare parts stocked in European warehouses; consumable cost drops about 60% against plasma.

### Questions the customer asked

**Q: Can it cut sheet and tube at the same time?**
No, it is a switchable all-in-one machine. The tube chuck retracts during sheet cutting. It suits a factory where sheet is the main work and tube is secondary, without buying two machines.

**Q: Can the 6 m tube length be extended?**
The standard model takes 6 m tubes, and 3 m or 9 m custom tube racks are available for long structural work.

**Q: Does it come with CE documents, and will customs be a problem?**
Complete CE and RoHS certificates are supplied with English documents and machine nameplates, so the machine clears Spanish ports and can be filed locally without issue.

**Q: What consumables does it need, and can they be bought in Spain?**
Nozzles, protective lenses, coolant and fibre laser protective sleeves. They are standard parts available at local laser accessory suppliers, or we ship a bulk set with the machine.

**Q: Can it cut special-shaped tubes?**
Yes, but that requires a system modification.

### A two-line alternative

For a factory running balanced sheet and tube volumes in parallel, the split setup of an RCL3015-3000W flatbed plus an RCL2400-3000W tube cutter runs both lines at once and roughly doubles total capacity.
