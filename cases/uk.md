## 3015 Sheet-and-Tube Fibre Laser for a UK Metal Processing Plant

Metal · controlled cost

`Laser 3000W` `Sheet-and-tube`

**Country**: UK

**Work**: Metal sheet and tube cutting

**Machine**: 3015 integrated sheet-and-tube fibre laser, 3000W

**The ask**: One machine, controlled equipment cost

### The situation

The plant processes both sheet and tube but had a limited budget, and a one-time purchase of two separate machines would have put too much pressure on the finances. The practical answer was a single machine that does both.

### What we recommended

A 3015 sheet-and-tube fibre laser at 3000W.

  * One unit replaces a flatbed and a tube cutter, which cuts the procurement cost significantly.
  * It frees up factory floor space compared with two machines.
  * Sheet and tube are handled in the same machine, so there is no cross-machine handling.
  * If tube orders grow later, a dedicated tube cutter can be added without throwing this machine away.

### Technical specifications

  * Cutting area: 1500 × 3000 mm
  * Laser brand: MAX MFSC series, Raycus or IPG
  * Cutting speed: 0-40,000 mm/min
  * Cooling: water cooling
  * Drive: Leadshine servo motors
  * Control: CypCut
  * Laser power: 3000W
  * Supported formats: AI, BMP, Dst, Dwg, DXF, DXP, PLT
  * Materials: various metals

### Questions the customer asked

**Q: What is the machine structure?**
It uses a split-type bed. Production and assembly are controlled so the bed stays parallel, which protects long-term cutting accuracy.

**Q: How do we maintain the chiller?**
Replace the internal circulating water every three months.

**Q: What control system does the fibre laser use?**
The Raytools X3S.

**Q: How many motors drive the tube-cutting tailstock?**
Two.

**Q: Does the machine have CE certification?**
Yes.

### Related machines

For sheet-only work, the RCL3015 flatbed is the simpler choice; for high tube volumes, a dedicated laser tube cutting machine is the way to go.
