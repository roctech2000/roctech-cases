# Industry Solutions

Eight materials and industries ROCTECH handles, each one tied to a customer who actually did it.

Most "solutions" pages are wish lists. This one isn't — every card below points to a real case in the [Customer Cases](cases/) section. If you're talking to a shop in that industry, you've got a proof point ready.

### Panel furniture / cabinets

Nesting centre → edge bander → 6-side drill as one line. The proof: a cabinet shop cut scrap from 18% to 7%, and ROCTECH wrote up a real visit to a shop that switched to their centre.

[See cases →](cases/)

### Solid wood / curved parts

5-axis RCF series machines chair backs and bent legs in one setup. The proof: a Romanian workshop has run ROCTECH woodworking machines for 10 years on carved pavilions and lattice work.

[Romania case →](cases/romania.md)

### Moulds / foam

RCF 5-axis plus the Big CNC for Molds (EPS) line. Car, aerospace, and sculpture foam moulds.

### Stone carving

Tiered setup from entry 3-axis to 5-axis. The hard parts are sealed rails, coolant, and spindle cooling — buy a stone machine, not a wood router with a stone bit.

### Signs / advertising

CNC Router for Sign Makers line; acrylic and PVC letters. The proof: a US makerspace runs an RC1530S-ATC doing sign engraving day in, day out.

[USA case →](cases/usa-reno.md)

### Metal / aluminium

Fibre lasers — flat, tube, sheet-and-tube, high power — plus CNC. The proof: eight laser cases across Malaysia, Spain, Italy, Mexico and more.

[Laser cases →](cases/)

### Education / makerspaces

RC series with DSP controller, no PC needed. The proof: the Reno makerspace's ROCTECH has survived years of different operators.

[USA case →](cases/usa-reno.md)

### Plastics / HDPE

HDPE line with chain feed and cleaning system. The proof: Guillermo in Chile runs one steadily and is coming back for loading/unloading systems.
