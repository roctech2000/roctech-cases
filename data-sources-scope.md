# Sources & Gaps

Where this knowledge base came from, what's solid, and what's still missing.

### What's in here (solid)

• 12 named customer cases — 4 woodworking/plastics, 8 metal lasers, across 12 countries.

• Quantified selling points — accuracy, reliability, material savings, power, compliance.

• The actual shipped laser model map (RCL / RC / ROCLAS).

• Buying / SEO keyword templates.

• Company facts, including the Chinese name (若泰) and certification years.

### What's still missing

• 16 of the 26 official FAQ questions aren't in the local records (only IDs 766-775 captured).

• Accuracy figures disagree: the site lists ±0.05 mm for the RCF1325, the FAQ claims 0.01 mm. Confirm per model before quoting.

• Q&A.doc (old binary format) wasn't parsed. The 10 long English articles were skimmed for structure, not fully absorbed.

• Forum and Reddit customer chatter (humor_faqs, forum_posts) wasn't folded in.

### Where the data came from

**A.** roc-tech.com — about-us, faq, product centre, newslist/1108·1109·1110, contact-us.

**B.** Local 若泰机器资料/案例文件_整理 — 12 case folders (EN docs + photos/video).

**C.** Local — roctech_faqs, CNC路由器采购关键词汇总.xlsx, roctech_10_articles.

Heads-up: laser power, price, and scrap rates are indicative from cases/articles. Quote from ROCTECH directly.

**Note on the brand split:** ROCTECH is the parent (Jinan Ruotai / 若泰). The laser line runs under the ROCLAS name. Both show up in the cases — the Irish shop, for example, crossed from ROCTECH woodworking machines to a ROCLAS laser.

This page is the audit trail. If something here looks off, check it against the source listed before you put it in front of a customer.
