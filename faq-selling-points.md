# Selling Points & Real Buying Scenarios

The numbers ROCTECH customers actually quote, plus the buying questions that come up most.

This page is for the conversation, not the catalogue. The figures below come from ROCTECH's own FAQ records and from the customer cases. Lead with the one that fits the customer in front of you.

## The numbers that close deals

### Accuracy

Mid-range routers quoted at 0.01 mm positioning, 0.005 mm repeat. The 5-axis is verified at 0.01 mm with the spindle spinning at 18,000 rpm under load — not measured while it's parked, which is how a lot of specs get fudged.

### Reliability

One customer ran a machine 10 hrs/day, 6 days/week for 7+ years and only ever replaced a $200 belt. ROCTECH backs it with 1 year of free parts, free first-year training, and 2-year warranty on core parts.

### Material savings

A cabinet shop took scrap from 18% down to 7% — about 61% less waste. At 40 sheets a week at $65 a sheet, that's roughly $16,900 a year back in material.

### Power & hidden cost

About 15-20% less power than comparable machines. CAM software alone runs $5k-15k a year, so bundle a spare-parts package at purchase and skip the panic later.

### Compliance you can show

CE (2014) and ISO 9001 (2018). ROCTECH supplies third-party load-accuracy reports, pre-ship test reports, and video factory tours so a buyer can verify before paying.

### Industry 4.0, lightly

Newer machines ship with vibration and temperature sensors, optional remote monitoring, and early warnings on spindle heat or axis vibration. Useful, not gimmicky.

## Real buying scenarios (from the FAQ)

These are actual questions people asked ROCTECH, with the answer that went out. Good for handling the same objection when it shows up again.

### Soft-metal memorial plaque

4"×6" half-inch brass: at least 300W and a rigid Z-axis. RC0609 with a 3.0 kW HSD spindle (~$3,500+). Brass engraving uses a 30-60° V-bit, 0.1-0.3 mm deep, with a thin WD-40 mist.

### High-school robotics team — aluminium

At least 1.5 kW, ball screw, 50 kg+ frame. RC0609 (3.0-6.5 kW, ~1450 kg) can qualify for STEM grants up to $5,000.

### Campus makerspace, 4×8

RC1325 + DSP controller + 3.0 kW HSD + Yaskawa + vacuum table + full enclosure. The DSP controller runs without a PC, which is the most stable setup for mixed operators.
